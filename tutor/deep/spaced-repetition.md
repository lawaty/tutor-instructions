# Spaced Repetition & Revision — Deep

> Loaded via `▶ READ tutor/deep/spaced-repetition.md` at Teaching Workflow step 13 and on revision checks.
> Applies to: Deep Learn Mode.

## Schedule

Initial review intervals after a topic is confirmed:
- Day 0: first learning (lesson + quiz)
- Day 1: first review prompt
- Day 3: second review
- Day 7: third review (1 week)
- Day 14: fourth review (2 weeks)
- Day 30: fifth review (1 month)
- Day 60: sixth review (2 months)
- Day 120: long-term retention check (4 months)

## Adjustment Rules

- Struggles during review → reset to earlier interval.
- Excels during review → skip to next longer interval.
- Topics used in real tasks count as implicit reviews.
- Integration quiz performance influences retention confidence.

## Revision Trigger Conditions

Recommend a revision session when:
1. **Study gap detected** (3+ days since last session).
2. **Review schedule due** (any topic past its next review date).
3. **Milestone completed** (user finishes a substantial section, 5+ topics).
4. **Before a new major section** (bridge with a refresher of related topics).
5. **Integration quiz gaps identified**.
6. **User request** (on-demand review).

## Adaptive Revision Strategy

- **Strong retention (90%+)**: mark "Strong", push next review out (double interval), include in integration quizzes.
- **Moderate (70–89%)**: mark "Moderate", keep standard interval, note weak points.
- **Weak (<70%)**: mark "Needs Attention", reset to shorter interval (e.g., 3 days), recommend re-read, add to next revision, check prerequisite cascade.

## Study Gap Handling

- 3+ days: acknowledge, offer review.
- 7+ days: stronger recommendation + 15-min warm-up.
- 14+ days: mandatory focused revision on the last few topics.

## Revision vs New Learning Balance

- Early stage (0–10 topics): 80% new / 20% revision.
- Mid stage (11–30): 60% / 40%.
- Advanced (30+): 50/50 or revision-heavy.
